Teaching Assistant, CS 110: Computer Science Fundamentals

Undergraduate course, Emory University, 2024 – 2025

I served as a teaching assistant for Emory's CS 110: Computer Science Fundamentals during the Fall 2024 and Spring 2025 semesters. The course introduces the fundamentals of programming in Python, especially for students coming from non-CS backgrounds. Responsibilities included creating and posting homework assignments and their grading rubrics, holding office hours, grading and giving feedback on student work (including regrade requests), and meeting one-on-one with students to work through homework issues.

Teaching Assistant, CS 312: Computing, AI, Ethics and Social Theory

Undergraduate course, Emory University, 2023

I served as a teaching assistant for Emory's CS 312: Computing, AI, Ethics and Social Theory during the Fall 2023 semester, which was taught by Dr. Kristin Williams. The course examines the social and ethical issues raised by new computing technologies, moving from philosophical approaches such as utilitarianism, deontology and virtue ethics to case studies of today's most pressing challenges. Responsibilities included teaching a class session on content moderation and freedom of speech, supporting group discussions, holding weekly office hours, and grading and giving written feedback on reflections, worksheets, midterms and final papers (including regrade requests). I also helped prepare the syllabus and course materials before the semester, and wrote an end-of-term report for the instructor on what worked, what needed improvement and how the course had changed since Fall 2022.

K-12 Language Assistant, English Immersion

Kindergarten through secondary, Colegio Los Olivos, Málaga, Spain, 2019

I served as an English language assistant at Colegio Los Olivos during the Spring 2019 semester while completing my TEFL certification with Meddeas. I supported kindergarten, primary, secondary and after-school classes. Responsibilities included teaching phonics and vocabulary through songs, stories and games, directing class plays, leading debates and presentation practice, supporting textbook lessons, and preparing students for Cambridge English exams. I also provided English tutoring for my host family.
