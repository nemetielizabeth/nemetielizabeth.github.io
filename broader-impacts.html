Broader Impacts

Community Outreach

I want people outside the field to see how data and AI shape their health, especially students and communities who are underrepresented in STEM. Most of my outreach happens through hands-on public events, from science festivals to museum booths. This work was recognized with the 2024 Emory BMI Department Graduate Student Award for Community Service.

  • OpenBMI (Co-founder & President, 2023 – present): I co-founded OpenBMI, Emory's biomedical informatics outreach group, to bring BMI to students and the public. We've run interactive booths at the Atlanta Science Festival, including "Does AI Recognize Me?" (2023) and "Biases in Pulse Oximetry" (2025), along with a BMI careers panel and a speaker luncheon for Atlanta International School students.
  • Association for Women in STEM (AWIS) (Volunteer, 2023 – present): I help run AWIS outreach booths at the Fernbank Museum and the Atlanta Science Festival, highlighting research by women in Emory's BMI department.
  • Women in STEM Bristol (Interdisciplinary Coordinator, 2020 – 2021): As an undergraduate, I co-led events that built an inclusive STEM community and raised the visibility of women in science.
Science Communication

Writing for a general audience helps me explain research clearly and share the people behind it.

  • Bare Bones STEM (Science Writer, 2025 – present): I write accessible science articles for Bare Bones STEM, Emory's graduate-led science communication initiative.
