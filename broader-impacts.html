Broader Impacts

Community Outreach

I want people outside the field to see how data and AI shape their health, especially students and communities who are underrepresented in STEM. Most of my outreach happens through hands-on public events, from science festivals to museum booths. This work was recognized with the 2024 Emory BMI Department Graduate Student Award for Community Service.

  • OpenBMI (Co-founder, 2023 – present; President, 2023 – 2026): I co-founded OpenBMI, Emory's biomedical informatics outreach group, to bring BMI to students and the public. We've run interactive booths at the Atlanta Science Festival, including "Does AI Recognize Me?" (2023) and "Biases in Pulse Oximetry" (2025), along with a BMI careers panel and a speaker luncheon for Atlanta International School students. Behind the scenes, I designed the group's logo and t-shirts, recruited new members at the Graduate School welcome fair, and led the transition to new leadership.
  • Association for Women in STEM (AWIS) (Volunteer, 2023 – present): I help run AWIS outreach booths at the Fernbank Museum and the Atlanta Science Festival, highlighting research by women in Emory's BMI department.
  • Women in STEM Bristol (Interdisciplinary Coordinator, 2020 – 2021): As an undergraduate, I co-led events that built an inclusive STEM community and raised the visibility of women in science.
Research Mentorship

Mentoring lets me pass on what I've learned about doing research, from framing a question to getting the work published. I've guided students through projects that led to co-authored work.

  • Sarper Yurtseven (Google Summer of Code, Master's student at Politecnico di Milano, 2024): I supervised Sarper's summer project on our automated gastric MRI segmentation pipeline, which led to a co-authored abstract at Digestive Disease Week (DDW) 2025.
  • Aravind Venkatachalam (Undergraduate researcher, Emory University, 2025): I introduced Aravind to research through regular meetings and hands-on tasks. He contributed to our scoping review Voice Diaries as Digital Biomarkers, published at MIE 2026.
Science Communication

Writing for a general audience helps me explain research clearly and share the people behind it.

  • Bare Bones STEM (Science Writer, 2025 – present): I write accessible science articles for Bare Bones STEM, Emory's graduate-led science communication initiative, including Early Detection, Lasting Impact: Saima Rathore on Improving Alzheimer's Care.
